Privacy Policy

Our commitment to patient confidentiality, transparent communication, and data protection at Dr. Nilofar's Dental Oasis.

PATIENT CONFIDENTIALITY & DIGITAL PRIVACY

How We Handle Your Information

At Dr. Nilofar's Dental Oasis, we are committed to upholding ethical medical confidentiality and safeguarding any personal details you share with us online or in person at our clinic in Moi, Pune.

Website Enquiries & WhatsApp

This static website prepares appointment request messages locally inside your web browser. We do not transmit, log, or store your appointment form entries or telephone numbers on an external web database.

Automated Clinic Assistant

Our on-page clinic assistant runs entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript keyword matching. It does not send chat messages to external AI servers, does not record conversations, and retains no personal profile data.

Third-Party Services

We provide Google Maps directions, Google Fonts typography, and direct WhatsApp links. When interacting with these features, technical connection data is handled according to Google's and WhatsApp's respective privacy policies.

In-Clinic Patient Records

This website does not store or access patient medical charts or clinical X-rays. Clinical records gathered during physical clinic visits are maintained offline at our clinic under doctor-patient confidentiality.

1. Website Enquiries & Appointment Requests

When you complete the appointment enquiry form on our website, the form data (such as your name, telephone number, selected treatment category, preferred date, and message) is compiled entirely on your device via client-side JavaScript. Clicking "Continue to WhatsApp" opens a secure pre-filled message directly in the WhatsApp application or WhatsApp Web addressed to our clinic front desk (+91 82082 62047).

Our website server does not intercept, log, or persist form submissions into any database. Once sent, your communication is transmitted directly through WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.) and is subject to WhatsApp's standard terms and privacy policies.

2. Cookies, Local Storage & Analytics

We respect your digital privacy and design our website with minimal technical overhead:

  • Cookies: This website does not set or read tracking cookies, marketing cookies, or profiling cookies.
  • Local Browser Storage (localStorage): We use local browser storage exclusively for functional user experience purposes:
    • dental-oasis-reviews-v1: Caches published clinic testimonials in memory for up to 24 hours to reduce unnecessary network requests.
    • dental-oasis-booked-slots-v1: Temporarily remembers chosen consultation time slots in your local browser session to prevent duplicate selections.
    No personal medical details, passwords, or identifying contact records are saved to your browser's local storage.
  • Analytics & Trackers: This website does not load third-party analytics SDKs, advertising pixels (such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel), or user session recording software.

3. Third-Party Integrations

To provide helpful clinic services, our website connects with trusted third-party providers:

  • Google Maps (Google LLC): Embedded on our contact page to display clinic directions to Vimal Complex, Moi. When the map loads, Google may receive your IP address and standard HTTP request headers in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC): Web typography (Plus Jakarta Sans and Montserrat) is delivered via Google's font servers.
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.): Enables direct front-desk chat and appointment scheduling confirmation.
  • External Links: Links to external platforms (including Google Business Profile, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and YouTube) direct you to third-party services that maintain independent privacy practices.

4. Clinical Records & Patient Confidentiality

This website serves solely as an informational and appointment coordination portal. It is not connected to an online Electronic Medical Record (EMR) system and does not store, host, or transmit clinical health charts.

Medical histories, dental examination notes, diagnostic photographs, and digital X-rays gathered during physical appointments at Dr. Nilofar's Dental Oasis are treated as confidential medical records under standard doctor-patient privilege and relevant Indian healthcare guidelines. Such records are accessible only to treating dental practitioners and authorized clinic personnel.
